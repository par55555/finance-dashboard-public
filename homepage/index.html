How these numbers are worked out
Net worth
Robinhood account values as Robinhood reports them and Robinhood bank balances, plus your Fidelity shares at live prices, your bank savings with interest, and your 401(k), minus card balances owed.
401(k)
The balance you gave, split by fund, each moved by its stand-in's price change since then. A stand-in tracks the same index or strategy as the plan fund but only approximates it, so the estimate can drift. Fees inside the plan aren't reflected.
Price
The most recent trade, including pre-market, after-hours and overnight sessions. Robinhood's own account values use the regular-session price, so holdings can differ slightly from the account totals.
Since last close
Latest price against the previous regular-session close. On weekends and evenings this is the extended-hours move.
Unrealized gain
Market value minus shares times Robinhood's average cost. Crypto is included; options are not held. Fidelity shares have no cost on file, so they're left out of gain until you add one.
Savings interest
The balance you gave, grown at its APY from that date to the time of the latest market quote. Banks usually credit interest monthly, so the bank's figure can trail this estimate by a few weeks of interest.
Left out
Deposits still in transit, and two delisted positions (ASAPQ, SESN^) that are worth effectively nothing.
